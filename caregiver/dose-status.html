About Dose Monitoring

This page enables caregivers to view medication updates shared by connected patients.

The dose status indicates whether a patient has recorded a dose as taken, missed, or still pending.

Caregivers should use this information to support patients with their medication routines. A missed dose should not automatically lead to taking an extra dose; patients should follow their prescribed medical advice.
